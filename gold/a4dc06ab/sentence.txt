The free height from the ground or from the surface of a driveway or walkway of the lower edge of a structural element, device or equipment protruding from the building, such as a balcony, bay window, sign, lighting device or awning, must be at least 2.2 metres, unless the area is protected so as to prevent the danger of collision.
